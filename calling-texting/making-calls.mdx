---
title: "Make Calls and Send Texts to Any Contact in CRE Fuel"
sidebarTitle: "Calls and Texts"
description: "Call or text any contact without switching apps. CRE Fuel logs every conversation automatically and ties it to the right contact record."
---

Every call and text you make in CRE Fuel is logged automatically to the contact's record. You never have to copy notes between apps or wonder if a follow-up happened. This page shows you how to reach a contact by phone or text in under thirty seconds.

## Making a Call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact record">
    Search for the contact's name in the top search bar, then click their name to open the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the phone icon">
    Find the phone icon next to the contact's number. Click it. CRE Fuel dials the number immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the call to connect">
    A call panel slides up at the bottom of your screen. You will hear ringing. Talk when the contact picks up. Click **End Call** when you are finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Calls go out showing **your own cell number**, the one your contacts already know. When they call back, it rings your cell.
</Info>

## Sending a Text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact record">
    Search for the contact and click their name to open the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the message icon">
    Find the message icon next to the contact's phone number. Click it. A text compose window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your message">
    Write your message in the text field. You can use a saved template by clicking **Templates** and selecting one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Click the **Send** button. The message is delivered and logged to the contact's activity feed right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also text from the **Conversations** tab. Go there when you want to see your full back-and-forth thread with a contact before you reply.
</Tip>
