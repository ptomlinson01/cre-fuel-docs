---

A guided call session is a focused block of calling where CRE Fuel does the organizing for you. It presents one contact at a time, shows a suggested script, logs the result of each call, and lines up the next contact automatically. You stay in the flow without clicking around or losing your place.

## Starting a Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Calling > Start Session">
    Click **Calling** in the left sidebar, then click **Start Session**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to call">
    Select a saved list or a contact tag to define your calling pool. For example, choose "Active Buyers" or "Cold Owners Q2."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start">
    Click **Start**. The session screen opens and loads your first contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the Session

CRE Fuel walks you through each contact one at a time. Here is what to do for each call:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Call">
    Click the **Call** button. CRE Fuel dials the contact using your CRE Fuel number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the script">
    A suggested script appears on the right side of the screen. Use it as a guide or adapt it to fit the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an outcome">
    After the call, click the outcome that matches what happened:
    - **Answered** - you spoke with the contact
    - **No Answer** - it rang with no pickup
    - **Voicemail** - you left a message
    - **Not Interested** - the contact asked to be removed

    CRE Fuel logs your choice to the contact's record automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a quick note">
    Type any key details in the note field. Keep it short: one or two sentences is enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Click **Next**. CRE Fuel loads the next contact and you keep going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your results">
    When you click **End Session** or finish your list, CRE Fuel takes you to a summary screen. Your call totals appear in **Your Numbers** and are added to your daily scorecard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Aim for at least 20 calls per session. That number moves your scorecard meaningfully and builds the habit of consistent daily outreach.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Contacts marked **Not Interested** during a session are automatically flagged in CRE Fuel. They will not appear in future sessions unless you change their status.
</Info>
