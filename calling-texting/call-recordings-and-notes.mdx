---

After every call, CRE Fuel saves a recording and gives you a place to write down what mattered. Reviewing a recording and adding a note takes two minutes. Doing it right after the call means nothing slips through the cracks.

## Listening to a Recording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish your call">
    End the call by clicking **End Call** in the call panel. CRE Fuel processes the recording automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the contact's activity feed">
    Go to the contact's record and scroll down to the **Activity** section. The recording appears there within about one minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Play">
    Click the **Play** button on the call log entry. The recording plays directly in the browser. Use the scrubber to jump to any point in the call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your state requires two-party consent for call recording, go to **Settings > Phone** and turn on the notification prompt. CRE Fuel will play a short disclosure at the start of every call.
</Note>

## Adding Call Notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the call log entry">
    On the contact's record, scroll to the **Activity** section and find the call you just completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Note">
    Click **Add Note** on that call log entry. A text field opens below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your notes">
    Write down the key points: what the contact said, what they need, and what you promised to do next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save**. The note is attached to that call entry and is now searchable across CRE Fuel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  While you are in the note, write a specific follow-up date. For example: "Call back Thursday to discuss listing price." Then set a task right there so it shows up on your scorecard.
</Tip>
