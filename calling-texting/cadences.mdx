A cadence is **how often you call a tag**. Tag a contact as a hot seller and they go on a two-day clock. Log the call and the clock resets. When they come due again, they show up on your call list.

<Note>
  **This is already on.** You do not build a cadence, assign it, or start it. Cadences ship switched on with sensible spacing, and tagging a contact is the only action you take.
</Note>

## What ships turned on

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Hot Seller · every 2 days">
    Ready to sell now or within about six months. No email campaign. You call these constantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="12-Month Seller · every 30 days">
    Might sell within the next year or two. Also on the 12-month email campaign, so calls stay light.
  </Card>
  <Card title="10-Year Seller · every 120 days">
    Does not want to sell. A long, light touch so you are top of mind whenever that changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it actually works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You tag the contact">
    Add `seller-hot`, `seller-12-mo`, or `seller-10-yr` to a contact. That is the whole setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The clock starts">
    CRE Fuel tracks when you last spoke to them and counts forward by that tag's spacing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They surface when due">
    Once they pass their interval, they appear on your call list to be worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logging the call resets it">
    Log the call and the clock starts again from that day. There is nothing to tick off and nothing to reschedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a contact carries more than one of these tags, the **most urgent one wins**. Someone tagged both hot and 12-month is treated as hot, so you never under-call a live seller because of a leftover tag.
</Tip>

## Changing the spacing

The numbers are defaults, not rules. If you work a hot seller every day rather than every second day, change it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Go to **Settings → Phone → Follow-up Cadences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the interval">
    Change the number of days for any cadence to match how you actually work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Setting an interval to zero turns that cadence **off**. Contacts with that tag will stop surfacing on your call list.
</Warning>

## Cadences are calls, campaigns are email

Two different systems, easy to confuse:

- **Cadences** decide how often a tagged contact comes back around for **you to call**.
- **Campaigns** send the **email and text** follow-up automatically. See [Nurture campaigns](/marketing/nurture-campaigns).

A 12-month seller is on both: the campaign keeps in touch in writing, and the cadence brings them back to your call list every 30 days.
