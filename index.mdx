## Get up and running fast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Open the invite email from CRE Fuel and click **Activate Account**. Set your password and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your phone number">
    Go to **Settings > Phone** and claim your CRE Fuel number. This is the number your contacts will see when you call or text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your contacts">
    Go to **Contacts** and click **Import**. Upload a spreadsheet or paste a CSV to bring in your existing list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first call session">
    Go to **Calling > Start Session** to work through your call list with guided scripts, automatic call logging, and one-click follow-up scheduling.
  </Step>
</Steps>
