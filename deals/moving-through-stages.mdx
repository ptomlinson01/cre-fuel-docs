Your pipeline is only useful if the stages are current. Every time something meaningful happens on a deal, move the stage forward. It takes about five seconds and keeps your scorecard and forecast accurate.

## Default pipeline stages

CRE Fuel comes with six default stages. Every deal in every pipeline starts at Prospect and works toward Closed.

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Prospect** | You have identified the lead but have not reached out yet |
| **Outreach** | You have made contact or left a message |
| **Meeting** | A meeting or site tour is scheduled or has happened |
| **Proposal** | You have sent a proposal, LOI, or listing agreement |
| **Under Contract** | Both parties have signed and the deal is in process |
| **Closed** | The transaction is complete |

## Move a deal to a new stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deal card">
    Click the deal card from the pipeline board or list view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the current stage">
    Find the stage indicator near the top of the card and click it. A dropdown appears showing all available stages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new stage">
    Click the stage you want to move the deal to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The card moves to the new column on your pipeline board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On the Kanban board view, you can also drag a deal card from one column to the next. The stage updates automatically when you drop it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Moving a deal to **Closed** triggers the commission entry step. CRE Fuel will prompt you to fill in closing details before the deal is marked complete. See [Closing and Commissions](/deals/closing-and-commissions) for the full walkthrough.
</Note>
