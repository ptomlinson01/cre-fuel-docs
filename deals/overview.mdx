CRE Fuel ships with three pipelines: **Sellers**, **Buyers**, and **Tenants**. Keeping them apart means your seller prospects never get mixed in with your buyer or tenant work, and each one can move through stages that actually fit that side of the business.

<Note>
  **A quick word on wording.** A *pipeline* is the container. A **lead** is the record inside it, and that is what you create. You will hear "deal" used loosely for the same thing, including in the **Active Deals** pipeline name, and that is fine. They are the same record.
</Note>

## Where to find them

- **Deals → Pipeline** shows the board, where you drag a lead from one stage to the next.
- **Deals → All deals & leads** shows the same work as a list, one row per lead.
- **Deals → Commissions** is where closed business turns into money owed. See [Closing and commissions](/deals/closing-and-commissions).

## Creating a lead

Click **+ New** in the top bar and choose **New Lead**. You pick the contact first, then the intake form opens for that type of work. Building a lead from a contact rather than from scratch is what keeps the call history, notes, and property attached to it.

## What a lead holds

- **Property address** — the specific property tied to the work
- **Contact** — the seller, buyer, or tenant it belongs to
- **Stage** — where it sits in its pipeline
- **Value** — the size of the transaction
- **Notes** — your own free text
- **Activity** — the running history of calls, texts, emails and updates

## The stages

All three pipelines run the same shape, so you only learn it once:

**New → Active → Under Contract → Closed**

<Warning>
  **Taking a listing is not a win.** Winning a listing puts the seller in *Active*, not *Closed*. Nothing counts as closed until it actually closes, which is what keeps your numbers honest.
</Warning>
