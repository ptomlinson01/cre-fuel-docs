CRE Fuel gives you three separate deal pipelines: Sellers, Buyers, and Tenants. Each pipeline lives in its own tab under the **Deals** section. Keeping them separate means your seller prospects never get mixed up with your buyer or tenant leads, and you always know exactly where each deal stands.

## What is a deal card?

Every deal you create gets its own deal card. The card holds all the key information for that deal in one place.

A deal card contains:

- **Property address** - the specific property tied to the deal
- **Contact** - the seller, buyer, or tenant linked to the deal
- **Stage** - where the deal currently sits in your pipeline
- **Deal value** - the estimated transaction value or commission
- **Notes** - your own free-text notes about the deal
- **Activity log** - a running history of calls, texts, emails, and updates

You can open any deal card with a single click from the pipeline board or list view.
