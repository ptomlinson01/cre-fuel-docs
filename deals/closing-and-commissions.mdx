When a deal crosses the finish line, you close it in CRE Fuel and record the commission. That data flows straight into your daily scorecard and annual totals on the dashboard. Skipping this step means your numbers will be off, so make it a habit to close deals the same day they settle.

## Close a deal and record commission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deal card">
    Click the deal card from your pipeline board or list view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the stage to Closed">
    Click the current stage indicator, select **Closed** from the dropdown, and confirm when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the closing date">
    Type or select the date the transaction closed in the **Closing Date** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the commission details">
    Type the **Gross Commission Amount**. If you are splitting the commission, enter your **Split Percentage** in the field below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the deal closed">
    Click **Mark Closed**. CRE Fuel saves the deal, logs the commission, and updates your scorecard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Closed deals count toward your daily scorecard and appear in the annual totals widget on your dashboard. You can see a running total of closings and gross commission for the year without doing any math yourself.
</Info>

<Tip>
  After closing, add a note to the deal card with the final sale price or key lease terms. You will thank yourself the next time a client asks for a comp or you need to refresh your memory on a deal.
</Tip>
