Blasts let you send a single message to a group of contacts at once. Use them for market updates, new listings, price reductions, or any time you need to reach a crowd quickly.

<Warning>
  **Email works out of the box. Texting has to be switched on first.**

  Sending texts requires a **one-time carrier registration**, under **Settings > Phone > Texting (SMS)**. This is the carriers' requirement for business messaging, not ours, and until it is done your texts will not deliver.

  Texting is a paid add-on. Calling is included.
</Warning>

## Send an Email Blast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Blast form">
    Go to **Marketing Blasts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Email">
    Select **Email** as the blast type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who it goes to">
    Choose a saved contact list from **Send to**. Everyone on that list receives the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Enter a **Subject** line, then write your email body in the editor below. Keep the subject under 60 characters so it displays fully in most inboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Click **Send** to deliver immediately, or click **Schedule** to pick a future date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Merge tags

Merge tags use **double curly braces**. `{{first_name}}` works; `{first_name}` does not, and it will send exactly as typed to everyone on your list.

**About the contact:**

`{{first_name}}` · `{{last_name}}` · `{{full_name}}` · `{{company}}` · `{{email}}` · `{{phone}}`

**About you**, so a template can be reused across a team without rewriting the sign-off:

`{{your_first_name}}` · `{{your_brokerage}}` · `{{your_email}}` · `{{your_market}}`

<Tip>
  Always send yourself a test before the real thing. It is the only way to catch a merge tag that did not fill in, and an email that opens "Hi ," is worse than one with no name at all.
</Tip>

<Info>
  CRE Fuel automatically appends an unsubscribe link to every email blast. Contacts who click it are removed from future emails so you stay compliant without any extra work.
</Info>

## Send a Text Blast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Blast form">
    Go to **Marketing Blasts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Text">
    Select **Text** as the blast type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who it goes to">
    Choose a saved contact list from **Send to**. Only contacts with a mobile number on file will receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Type your message in the text field. Stay under 160 characters to send as a single SMS and avoid split messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send** to deliver immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Text blasts only go to contacts who have a mobile phone number saved on their contact record. If a contact is missing that field, they will be skipped. Check the recipient count before sending to spot any gaps.
</Warning>

<Info>
  CRE Fuel automatically appends STOP opt-out instructions to every text blast. Contacts who reply STOP are unsubscribed from future texts right away.
</Info>
