Blasts let you send a single message to a group of contacts all at once. Use them for market updates, new listings, price reductions, or any time you need to reach a crowd quickly. CRE Fuel handles delivery and compliance automatically.

## Send an Email Blast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Blast form">
    Go to **Marketing** in the left sidebar, then click **New Blast**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Email">
    Select **Email** as the blast type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your audience">
    Choose a saved audience or select a tag from the **Audience** dropdown. Everyone in that group will receive the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Enter a **Subject** line, then write your email body in the editor below. Keep the subject under 60 characters so it displays fully in most inboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Click **Send** to deliver immediately, or click **Schedule** to pick a future date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add `{first_name}` anywhere in your subject or body. CRE Fuel replaces it with each contact's first name automatically. Personalized messages get noticeably higher response rates.
</Tip>

<Info>
  CRE Fuel automatically appends an unsubscribe link to every email blast. Contacts who click it are removed from future emails so you stay compliant without any extra work.
</Info>

## Send a Text Blast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Blast form">
    Go to **Marketing** in the left sidebar, then click **New Blast**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Text">
    Select **Text** as the blast type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your audience">
    Choose a saved audience from the **Audience** dropdown. Only contacts with a mobile number on file will receive the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Type your message in the text field. Stay under 160 characters to send as a single SMS and avoid split messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send** to deliver immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Text blasts only go to contacts who have a mobile phone number saved on their contact record. If a contact is missing that field, they will be skipped. Check your audience size before sending to spot any gaps.
</Warning>

<Info>
  CRE Fuel automatically appends STOP opt-out instructions to every text blast. Contacts who reply STOP are unsubscribed from future texts right away.
</Info>
