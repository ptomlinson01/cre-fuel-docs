A campaign is a sequence of messages that goes out over time, so a relationship stays warm without you remembering to write anything.

Everything lives in one place: **Marketing > Campaigns**. Nurture campaigns are not a separate section.

## Two seller nurtures come with your account

**You do not have to build these.** They arrive written, and they are already personalised with your name and your market.

| Campaign | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Seller 10-Year** | The long game. An owner who bought recently is not selling this quarter, but they will sell eventually, and the broker still in front of them then is the one who gets the call. |
| **Seller 12-Month** | The nearer horizon, for owners closer to a decision. |

<Warning>
  **Both ship dormant.** Nothing sends until you deliberately turn a campaign on. Your contacts will not receive anything you did not switch on yourself.
</Warning>

## They are already in your voice

The copy uses merge tokens that fill in from your sender profile when a message actually sends: your first name, your brokerage, your market. There is no generic sign-off to find and replace, and nothing in them belongs to anyone else.

<Tip>
  Read one before you switch it on. Not because it needs fixing, but because you should know exactly what is going out over your name.
</Tip>

## Editing one makes it yours

Edit a shipped campaign and your version is saved as **your** copy. It overrides the original from then on.

Anything you have not touched keeps rendering from the current version, so improvements to the built-in copy reach you automatically. The moment you edit, that campaign is yours and it stops changing underneath you.

<Note>
  This is a deliberate trade. Untouched campaigns stay current; edited ones stay exactly as you left them. You never get a surprise rewrite of copy you had already tuned.
</Note>

## The pace is deliberately slow

The 10-year sequence waits **two months** before the first message, then roughly **four months** between touches after that.

That feels slow if you are used to marketing cadences. It is right for this job. An owner ten years from selling does not want to hear from you monthly, and a broker who shows up quarterly for a decade is remembered, while one who shows up weekly for a month gets filtered.

<Tip>
  Nothing stops you building your own campaigns alongside these. The two seller nurtures are a floor, not a ceiling.
</Tip>
