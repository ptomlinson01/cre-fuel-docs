Your book is your business. Here is a straight answer about where it lives and who can get to it.

## Your data is not sitting on somebody's laptop

CRE Fuel runs on managed cloud infrastructure in the United States. There is no server under a desk, no synced folder, and no single computer whose loss or theft would expose your contacts.

That includes ours. If every machine at CRE Fuel died tomorrow, your data would be untouched and the app would stay up. That is a deliberate design rule, not a happy accident.

## The companies that hold it are independently audited

CRE Fuel is a layer on top of infrastructure run by established providers. Those providers carry the certifications an IT department looks for:

They also run annual third-party penetration testing, encrypt your data both while it is stored and while it moves, and require two-factor authentication for their own staff.

## What CRE Fuel does on top of that

- **Your password is never stored.** Not by us, not anywhere. We keep a one-way scrambled version that cannot be turned back into your password. Nobody at CRE Fuel can see it, including us.
- **Your account is separate from every other broker's.** Each brokerage has its own isolated account. There is no shared list where your contacts sit next to someone else's.
- **Permissions are enforced on the server.** If a team member is limited to their own contacts, or blocked from seeing commissions, that limit is real. It is not a hidden menu they could get around by editing a web address.
- **Two-factor authentication is available** on every account, and we recommend turning it on. See [Two-Factor Authentication](/security/two-factor).
