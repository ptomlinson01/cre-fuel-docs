You need to add a new contact and be able to find any contact quickly. This page covers both actions.

## Adding a New Contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New Contact">
    Click the **+ New Contact** button in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Enter the contact's Name, Phone, Email, Company, and Property Type. Add any Notes you already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save**. The contact now appears in your list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If someone calls you and they're not in your contacts yet, you can add them directly from the call log. Open the call entry and click **Add to Contacts**. Their phone number will be pre-filled.
</Tip>

## Searching Your Contact List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the search bar">
    Click the search bar at the top of the Contacts page and type a name, phone number, company, or email address. Results update as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by tag or property type">
    Click the **Filter** button next to the search bar. Select one or more tags or property types. CRE Fuel shows only contacts that match all selected filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear filters">
    Click **Clear Filters** to return to your full contact list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
