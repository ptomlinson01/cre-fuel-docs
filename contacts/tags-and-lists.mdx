Tags let you group contacts any way that makes sense for your business. You create the labels, you apply them, and you can filter or message by them instantly. A well-tagged list saves hours of manual sorting every week.

## What Tags Are

A tag is a short label you attach to one or more contacts. Tags are free-form, so you can use anything that fits your workflow. Common examples:

- Seller
- Buyer
- Hot Lead
- Office
- Industrial
- Follow Up This Week

One contact can have as many tags as you need.

## Add a Tag to a Contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact record">
    Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then click the contact's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Tags field">
    Scroll to the **Tags** field in the contact record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your tag">
    Click inside the Tags field and type a new tag name, or click an existing tag from the dropdown to apply it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The tag is now attached to that contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter Contacts by Tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    Click the **Filter** button next to the search bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a tag">
    Under the Tags section, click the tag you want to filter by. CRE Fuel immediately shows only contacts with that tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Combine tags">
    Click additional tags to narrow the list further. CRE Fuel returns contacts that match all selected tags.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Tags are also how you build the lists a blast goes to. Any tag you create here is available when you filter and save a list on this screen.
</Note>
