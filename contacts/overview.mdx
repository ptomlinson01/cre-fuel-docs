Your contacts are the foundation of everything you do in CRE Fuel. Every person you call, text, or email lives here. A clean, well-organized contact list means faster follow-up, better marketing, and fewer deals that slip through the cracks.

## What's in a Contact Record

| Field | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Name** | First and last name of the contact |
| **Phones** | You can hold more than one, each labelled **Mobile**, **Work**, **Home**, or a label you type yourself |
| **Email** | Used for campaigns and direct email |
| **Company** | The firm or ownership entity they represent |
| **Contact Type** | What this person *is* to your business: Buyer, Investor, Owner User, Developer, and so on |
| **Vendor Type** | For service providers rather than principals: title company, attorney, lender, contractor, property management |
| **Asset Type** | The asset classes they buy, sell, or lease. A buyer can have several, with one marked primary |
| **Addresses** | Each labelled **Work**, **Home**, **Investment**, or a custom label |
| **Lead Source** | Where the relationship came from |
| **Tags** | Labels for grouping and filtering, and what puts a seller on a call clock |
| **Notes** | Free-form notes from calls, meetings, or research |
| **Last Contacted** | Updated for you whenever you log a call, text, or email |

<Note>
  **Contact Type is a field. Where they are in your process is a tag.** The type barely changes over a relationship's life, so it stays a dropdown. The stage they are at changes constantly, so it stays a tag. Keeping them apart is why filtering works the way it does.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Last Contacted is what drives drift.** It updates itself when you log a touch, and CRE Fuel reads it to work out who has gone quiet. You never set it by hand. See [Drift and follow-ups](/your-numbers/drift-and-follow-ups).
</Tip>

<Warning>
  A few tags beginning with `sys-` are managed by CRE Fuel to mirror your dropdown selections. They are hidden from you on purpose. Leave them alone and let the dropdowns drive them.
</Warning>
