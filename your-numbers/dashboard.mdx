The dashboard is your home base. Every time you open CRE Fuel, it shows you a live snapshot of your activity so you can decide where to focus without digging through menus or doing math in your head.

## What the Dashboard Shows

The dashboard has six widgets arranged across the screen. Each one tracks a different part of your business.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Calls Today">
    The number of outbound calls you have made since midnight. This resets every day at 12:00 AM your local time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Texts Today">
    The number of texts you have sent today. Includes both manual texts and texts sent from a campaign sequence.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New Contacts This Week">
    Every contact you added to CRE Fuel since Monday. Helps you see whether your prospecting pipeline is growing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deals in Pipeline">
    Your active deals grouped by stage: Prospect, LOI, Under Contract, and Closed. Click any stage to open that filtered deal list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Commissions This Month">
    The total projected commissions from deals currently in your pipeline that are expected to close this calendar month.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Commissions This Year">
    Your year-to-date projected commissions. Pulls from all deals marked Closed plus open deals expected to close before December 31.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Widget Details

### Calls Today

This widget counts every call you logged or dialed through CRE Fuel today. It does not count calls made outside the app. If a number seems low, check that you are logging calls rather than dialing from your phone directly.

### Texts Today

This includes every text thread you started or replied to today inside CRE Fuel. Bulk campaign texts count toward this number as well.

### New Contacts This Week

A rising number here means your top of funnel is healthy. A flat number for several days in a row is a signal to spend more time prospecting.

### Deals in Pipeline by Stage

Each stage shows a count and a total deal value. The stages run left to right from earliest to latest:

1. **Prospect** - You are in early conversations.
2. **LOI** - A letter of intent is out or being negotiated.
3. **Under Contract** - The deal is in due diligence.
4. **Closed** - The deal has funded and you have earned the commission.

Click any stage name to jump to a filtered view of just those deals.

### Commissions This Month and This Year

These numbers are projections based on the commission amounts you entered on each deal card. They are not confirmed revenue. Update deal amounts regularly to keep these figures accurate.

<Tip>
  Check the dashboard at the start of your day to set your priorities, then again at the end of the day to see what you actually did. Two minutes of review each morning and evening builds a habit of staying on top of your numbers.
</Tip>
