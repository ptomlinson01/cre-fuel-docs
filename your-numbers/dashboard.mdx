The dashboard is not a wall of widgets. It is **three zones, in order, answering the three questions a broker actually asks each morning.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Am I winning?">
    A slim scoreboard strip at the top: your Verdict, your lead pace, and your buyer book. Plain English, not a chart to interpret. This is the "am I ahead, behind, or crushing it" answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What should I do right now?">
    **This is the hero of the screen.** One merged action queue telling you who to contact today. Not a report, a list of people to work through, in order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where am I drifting?">
    The Drift board: the dropped balls. People who have gone quiet, follow-ups past their date, things waiting on you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The rule that keeps it usable

**Nothing shows in two places.**

- **Due today** lives in zone 2. That is your plan for today.
- **Already past its time** — overdue, unanswered, gone cold — lives in zone 3. That is Drift.

The moment a due-today item goes past its date, it stops being your plan and becomes a dropped ball, so it moves from zone 2 to zone 3. It never sits in both.

<Info>
  This is why there is no separate activity feed. There used to be one and it was retired, because it duplicated Drift's "waiting" list. Two places showing the same overdue contact meant clearing it in one place left it staring at you from the other.

  This dashboard replaced a pile of about seven cards that had grown to overlap each other. Fewer answers, each in exactly one place, beats more widgets.
</Info>

## Why it does not open on your numbers

Production totals, closings, and commission are on the [Scorecard](/your-numbers/scorecard), not here.

That is deliberate. Your year-to-date number is not something you can act on at 8am, and leading with it means opening the app to a verdict on your past instead of a plan for your day. Zone 1 gives you enough to know where you stand. Zone 2 tells you what to do about it.

<Tip>
  Work top to bottom and the screen is doing its job. If you find yourself scrolling to zone 3 first, that is worth noticing — it usually means the follow-ups got away from you and the queue in zone 2 is not being cleared.
</Tip>
