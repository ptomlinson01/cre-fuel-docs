The Scorecard answers one question: **am I on track for the year?** It sits on the Commissions screen and compares what you have actually closed against the production goals you set.

<Note>
  The Scorecard is a money screen. If you are on an Agent role rather than the account owner or an Admin, it will not be available to you.
</Note>

## Where to find it

Open **Deals → Commissions** in the left sidebar. The Scorecard is at the top of that screen, above the list of what you are owed.

## What it shows

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="My gross GCI">
    The commission that is yours, before your brokerage takes its share.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Total GCI">
    The full commission on the deal, including any co-broke split.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Units">
    How many deals you have closed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each of these is shown against the goal you set, so you can see the gap at a glance rather than working it out.

## Set your production goals

You only enter a few numbers. CRE Fuel works out the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Goals card">
    On **Deals → Commissions**, find the Goals card on the Scorecard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your drivers">
    Type in how many **deals** you want to close this year, your **average deal size**, and **your commission** percentage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it do the maths">
    From those three numbers CRE Fuel derives your volume and GCI targets. You do not have to calculate them yourself, and you do not have to keep them in step by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also reach the same goals from **Settings → Goals & Targets → Commission Goals**.

<Tip>
  Set the drivers you can actually influence. You control how many deals you go after and roughly what size you chase; you do not directly control a GCI number. Getting the drivers right is what makes the target believable.
</Tip>

## What the Scorecard is not

The Scorecard is a **yearly production** view. It is deliberately kept apart from your daily screen.

- For **what do I do right now**, use the [Dashboard](/your-numbers/dashboard). Its top zone answers "am I winning" against whichever metrics you have chosen, for whichever period you have selected.
- For **who has gone quiet**, use [Drift and follow-ups](/your-numbers/drift-and-follow-ups).

Keeping them separate is on purpose, so the screen you open every morning never turns into a reporting screen.
