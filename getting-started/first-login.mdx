---
title: "Activate Your CRE Fuel Account and Log In for the First Time"
sidebarTitle: "First Login"
description: "Activate your CRE Fuel account, set your password, and explore the dashboard so you can start calling and texting contacts right away."
---

When your account is created, CRE Fuel sends you an invitation email. Follow the steps below to activate your account and reach the dashboard. The whole process takes under two minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invite email">
    Look for an email from **noreply@crefuel.co** with the subject line "You're invited to CRE Fuel." Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Activate Account">
    Inside the email, click the **Activate Account** button. This opens CRE Fuel in your browser and takes you to the password-setup screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Type a new password in the **Password** field. Type it again in the **Confirm Password** field. Click **Save Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    You are now logged in. The main dashboard is your daily cockpit. You will see your scorecard, your pipeline summary, and your contact activity feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your activation link has expired or you forget your password later, go to the CRE Fuel login page and click **Forgot Password**. A reset link will arrive in your email within a minute.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **There is no App Store download.** You put CRE Fuel on your phone by opening it once and adding it to your home screen. You get a flame icon that opens full-screen and behaves like a normal app. It takes about 30 seconds.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open app.crefuel.co on your phone">
      Use Safari on iPhone, or Chrome on Android.
    </Step>
    <Step title="iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen">
      The Share button is the square with an arrow coming out of it. Scroll down the list, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Android: tap the three-dot menu">
      Choose **Add to Home screen** (some phones say **Install app**), then tap **Add**.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  It is the same CRE Fuel you use on your computer, so everything is already there. Nothing separate to set up, and nothing to keep in sync.
</Tip>
