CRE Fuel is a daily cockpit built for solo commercial real estate brokers and small brokerage teams. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, a separate dialer, and a generic CRM, you work from one focused screen. Every feature is designed around what you actually do each day: reach out to contacts, move deals forward, match buyers to listings, and track whether you hit your numbers.

## What CRE Fuel Does

- **Contacts** - Store, search, and tag everyone in your network.
- **Calling and Texting** - Dial and text from a dedicated CRE Fuel phone number without touching your personal line.
- **Deal Pipeline** - Track every active deal from first conversation to closed.
- **Buyer Matching** - Surface the right buyers when a new listing comes in.
- **Marketing Campaigns** - Send targeted emails and texts to your contact lists.
- **Daily Scorecard** - Set a call target and watch your progress update in real time.
