---
title: "Import Your Existing Contacts into CRE Fuel in Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Import Contacts"
description: "Upload a CSV or spreadsheet to bring your existing contact list into CRE Fuel quickly without re-entering names and numbers by hand."
---

If you already have contacts in a spreadsheet, another CRM, or an exported file, you can bring them all into CRE Fuel at once using the import tool. You do not need to add contacts one by one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the left navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    In the top-right area of the Contacts page, click the **Import** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Click **Choose File** and select your spreadsheet or CSV from your computer. Then click **Upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your columns">
    CRE Fuel shows a column-mapping screen. Use the dropdowns to match each column in your file to the correct CRE Fuel field. For example, match your "Full Name" column to the **Name** field and your "Cell" column to the **Phone** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    Once your columns are mapped, click **Import**. CRE Fuel loads your contacts. A confirmation screen shows how many records were added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The only required columns are **Name** and at least one of **Phone** or **Email**. All other columns are optional. If a column in your file does not match any CRE Fuel field, you can skip it during mapping.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="What file formats are supported?">
  CRE Fuel accepts the following formats for contact imports:

  - **CSV** (.csv) - the most reliable format and works with any spreadsheet app
  - **Excel** (.xlsx) - exported directly from Microsoft Excel
  - **Google Sheets export** - go to File > Download > Comma Separated Values (.csv) in Google Sheets and upload the resulting file

  Files must be under 10 MB. If your file is larger, split it into multiple imports.
</Accordion>
