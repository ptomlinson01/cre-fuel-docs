---
title: "Set Up Your CRE Fuel Phone Number"
sidebarTitle: "Phone Setup"
description: "Claim a dedicated local phone number in CRE Fuel so your contacts see a familiar area code when you call or text them."
---

Before you start calling or texting contacts, you need to claim a CRE Fuel phone number. This gives you a dedicated line that keeps your personal cell number private and logs every conversation inside CRE Fuel automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Phone">
    Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then click **Settings**. In the left sidebar, click **Phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your area code">
    In the **Area Code** field, type the three-digit area code you want. Pick one that matches your market so contacts recognize the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a number">
    CRE Fuel shows a list of available numbers for that area code. Click the number you want to select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm">
    Click the **Confirm** button. Your new number is now active. Any calls or texts you make from CRE Fuel will come from this number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  All calls and texts in CRE Fuel go through this number, including calls from the mobile app. Replies from your contacts come back to this number and appear in the contact's activity feed.
</Info>

<Note>
  If you already have a business number you want to keep, contact CRE Fuel support to ask about porting. Number porting is available but requires a separate process and takes a few business days to complete.
</Note>
