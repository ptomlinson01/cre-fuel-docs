---
title: "Connect Your Phone So You Can Call and Text"
sidebarTitle: "Connect Your Phone"
description: "Connect your cell to CRE Fuel once and your calls go out showing your own number. It takes about 30 seconds and it is the one thing to do before anything else."
---

This is the first thing to do in CRE Fuel, and it takes about 30 seconds. Until your phone is connected, the Call button cannot dial.

**You do not pick a phone number, and you do not get a new one.** Your calls go out showing **your own cell number**, the one your contacts already know. When someone calls back, it rings your cell.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Phone > Your Phone">
    Click your profile icon in the top-right corner, then **Settings**. Choose **Phone**, then **Your Phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your cell number">
    Type the mobile number you want your contacts to see. This should be the number you actually answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it">
    CRE Fuel sends you a short code. Enter it to confirm the number is yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the page says **Your phone is connected**, you are done. The Call button now works everywhere in the app.

<Note>
  Verifying proves you own the number. It is a one-time step, and it is the reason nobody but you can place calls that appear to come from your line.
</Note>

<Info>
  **Why there is no number to choose.** Some CRMs hand you a second phone number and ask you to pick an area code. CRE Fuel deliberately does not.

  A second number means your contacts see an unfamiliar caller, you have two places to check messages, and callbacks ring somewhere you are not sitting. Connecting your own cell means your contacts see the number they already have for you, and you keep answering the phone you already carry.
</Info>

<Accordion title="Will my contacts see my personal cell number?">
  They see the number you verified. Most brokers verify the cell they already give clients, which is the whole point: the call looks like it came from you, because it did.

  If you carry a separate business line, verify that one instead. Whatever you verify is what shows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do my calls still get logged?">
  Yes. Every call placed from CRE Fuel is logged on the contact's timeline with the date, who made it, and how long it lasted, whether you dialed from your computer or your phone.

  Connecting your cell changes what your contact sees on their screen. It does not change what CRE Fuel records.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if the code never arrives?">
  Check the number for a typo first, since that is the usual cause, and make sure it is a mobile number that can receive a text.

  If it still does not arrive, use **Get help** in your profile menu and say which number you tried.
</Accordion>
