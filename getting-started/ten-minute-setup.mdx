title: "Get CRE Fuel Ready in 10 Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "10-Minute Setup"
description: "Follow this five-step checklist to activate your account, set up your phone number, load your contacts, and run your first call session today."
---

You can go from a brand-new account to a fully working cockpit in a single sitting. This page walks you through every step in order. When you finish, you will have a phone number, a contact list, a daily call target, and your first call session underway.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate your account">
    Open the invitation email from CRE Fuel, click **Activate Account**, and set your password. You will land on the main dashboard.

    Need more detail? See [First Login](/getting-started/first-login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your phone number">
    Click your profile icon, then go to **Settings > Phone**. Choose an area code, pick a number from the list, and click **Confirm**.

    Need more detail? See [Phone Setup](/getting-started/setup-your-phone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the left menu, then click **Import**. Upload your CSV or spreadsheet, map your columns, and click **Import**.

    Need more detail? See [Import Contacts](/getting-started/import-contacts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your daily call target">
    Go to **Settings > Scorecard**. In the **Daily Call Target** field, type the number of calls you want to make each day. Click **Save**. This number appears on your dashboard scorecard and updates as you dial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first call session">
    Click **Contacts** in the left menu. Use the filters or search to find a group of contacts you want to reach today. Click the first contact, then click **Call**. CRE Fuel connects the call through your new number and logs it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can always go back and fill in more details later. Getting the basics set up now means you can start making calls today instead of spending hours configuring everything perfectly.
</Tip>
