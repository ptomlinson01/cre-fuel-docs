title: "Customize Your Pipelines and Stages in CRE Fuel"

---

Your pipelines live in **Settings > Customization > Pipelines**. Only the account owner can change them.

## Add a stage

Every pipeline starts with four stages: New, Active, Under Contract and Closed / Won. If you work in more steps, add them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pipelines">
    Go to **Settings > Customization > Pipelines**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add stage">
    It's under the stages of the pipeline you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and place it">
    Type the name, like *Tour Scheduled*, and pick the stage it **goes after**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it counts as">
    Choose **New**, **Active** or **Under Contract**, then click **Add stage**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new column shows up on your board right away. Deals already in your pipeline don't move.

## What "counts as" means

CRE Fuel reads what a stage **counts as**, not what it's called. A *Tour Scheduled* stage that counts as Active is treated exactly like Active everywhere: your Verdict, Leads, the deal card's next step, and how often CRE Fuel nudges you to call. A *Due Diligence* stage that counts as Under Contract counts toward your accepted offers and gets the escrow checklist.

Pick the stage your new step is a part of. You can change it any time from the dropdown on the stage's row.

## Rename a stage or pipeline

Click the pencil next to any stage or pipeline. A renamed stage keeps what it counts as, so nothing downstream changes.

<Warning>
  **The last stage has to keep "closed" or "won" in its name.** That's how CRE Fuel knows a deal moved there is won and asks you for the commission. For the same reason, a working stage can't use the words closed, won or lost.
</Warning>

## What you can't do here

Stages can't be deleted or reordered. Deleting a stage would strand every deal sitting in it. If you stop using a stage, leave it empty or rename it.

## Add a pipeline

Click **New pipeline**. It starts with the same four stages. Then set **Tracks as** to Seller work, Buyer work or Tenant work so its deals count toward your commissions and show in Leads. A pipeline set to Standalone is a working board that doesn't feed your numbers.
