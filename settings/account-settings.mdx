Your account settings control everything from your display name to how your phone line behaves. Keeping them current takes a few minutes and saves a lot of confusion later.

## Profile

Set your name, contact email, and time zone here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile settings">
    Go to **Settings > Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit your name, email address, and time zone as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set your time zone correctly. Your daily scorecard resets at midnight in your local time. If the time zone is wrong, your numbers will roll over at the wrong hour.
</Tip>

---

## Phone

View your dedicated CRE Fuel number and manage call recording settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Phone settings">
    Go to **Settings > Phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your CRE Fuel number">
    Your assigned number is displayed at the top of this page. This is the number contacts will see when you call or text them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle call recording">
    Flip the **Call Recording** toggle on or off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle two-party consent notification">
    Flip the **Two-Party Consent Notification** toggle on or off. When enabled, callers hear a brief disclosure that the call may be recorded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Laws around call recording vary by state. Check your local requirements before turning off the two-party consent notification.
</Note>

## Notifications

Choose which events send you an alert and how you receive it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications settings">
    Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your preferences">
    For each alert type below, choose **Email**, **Push**, both, or neither.

    | Alert | What it means |
    |---|---|
    | New Text Reply | A contact replied to one of your texts |
    | Follow-Up Due | A scheduled follow-up is due today |
    | Deal Stage Update | A deal moved to a new pipeline stage |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password

Change your password at any time from the Security tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Go to **Settings > Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the password change">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the prompts">
    Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Click **Update Password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After changing your password, you will be signed out of all other active sessions. Sign back in on your phone or other devices.
</Warning>
